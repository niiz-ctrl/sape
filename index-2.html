The idea

Not another English course.

SAPE adds a practical language layer to professional training without turning the vocational classroom into a conventional language classroom.

01

Recognise

Meet the English term where it naturally appears: software, equipment, documentation, warnings and workplace instructions.

02

Understand

Connect the term to an operational Arabic meaning and a precise professional context.

03

Use

Move from recognition toward practical interpretation through authentic workplace situations.

04

Transfer

Build familiarity that can support mobility beyond the local workplace and into international technical environments.

Module 01 · Database Administration
Content & Term Selection Standard

Quality starts before the first screen is built.

SAPE-CTS is the control layer for content creation. It provides a repeatable route from the official vocational reference environment to a defensible operational term set.

01

Reference

Start from the relevant Algerian vocational curriculum, competency or professional reference.

02

Extract

Identify terminology genuinely connected to tasks, equipment, documents and professional situations.

03

Filter

Apply SAPE-CTS criteria and remove decorative, generic or weakly relevant vocabulary.

04

Validate

Check meaning, terminology, Arabic operational equivalence and professional context.

05

Deploy

Turn the validated matrix into the learner and facilitator experience.

The direction

From a classroom experiment to a coherent professional system.

SAPE is being developed as a disciplined complementary layer: field-informed, curriculum-conscious, evidence-oriented and designed around the actual language demands of professional environments.
